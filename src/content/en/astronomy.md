## Tracking the ISS by hand

The ISS series used a Philips ToUCam on the 10-inch Dobsonian, **tracked by hand using the finder**. I compared seeing the station at roughly 400 km altitude with watching a small ant scurrying very quickly at the far end of a living room: a tiny apparent target moving quickly across the sky.

## Deep sky with Watec 120N

For sensitive live deep-sky imaging I used a **Watec 120N, also known as Stellacam II**, on the Dobsonian. M82 and M3 illustrate the live results. M51 provides a comparison of untracked and tracked exposures.

## Home-built equatorial platform

I built an equatorial platform for my **Galaxy D10 Dobsonian** in less than a week, adopting **Reiner Vogel’s VNS (Virtual North Segment)** approach. Even before full optimization it worked well for planetary tracking and deep-sky video astronomy. See [Reiner Vogel’s platform construction](https://www.reinervogel.net/index.html?/Plattform/Plattform.html).
